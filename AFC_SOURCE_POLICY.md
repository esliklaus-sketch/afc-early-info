# AFC research source maintenance

When researching an AFC match for the user, preserve each useful source in afc_extra_sources.json after checking its identity and reusable news URL. Prefer primary club and federation reporting. Add local-language team aliases and locale. Preserve existing entries. Do not confuse Qadsia Kuwait with Al Qadsiah Saudi or Al Ittihad Aleppo with Al Ittihad Saudi.

A source identified on the web is not proof of automatic retrieval. Report blocked pages, social sign-in walls, feed errors and unread articles honestly. Never remove relevance, date or evidence checks just to increase alert counts. Test additions without Telegram delivery. GitHub changes require a separate VPS update unless automatic deployment has been verified.
